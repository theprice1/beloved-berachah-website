Need Immediate Help?

Beloved Berachah provides ongoing support and workshops, but we are not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, please call 999. For 24/7 support, contact the organizations below.

Domestic Abuse Helpline

Free, 24-hour national helpline run by Refuge.

0808 2000 247

Samaritans

Whatever you're going through, a Samaritan will face it with you 24/7.

116 123

Mind Mental Health

Information and signposting service for mental health support.

0300 123 3393

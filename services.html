Services & Workshops

Equipping communities, churches, and organisations to support women navigating trauma and recovery.

How We Help

Through practical training, advocacy, and strategic partnerships, we work to remove the stigma surrounding addiction and trauma.

Education

Speaking Engagements

Our Founder, Brenda Fraser, delivers impactful talks combining lived experience with professional insights. Designed for churches, charities, and public sector organizations, these sessions educate audiences on the realities of addiction and domestic abuse while inspiring actionable change.

  • ✦ Awareness & Stigma Reduction
  • ✦ Personal Testimonies
Training

Trauma-Informed Workshops

Specialized training workshops equipping staff, volunteers, and faith group leaders with the practical tools needed to interact safely and compassionately with women experiencing trauma. We focus on bridging the gap between clinical therapy and community support.

  • ✦ Best Practice Approaches
  • ✦ Safe Signposting & Resources
Advocacy

Community Partnerships

We collaborate directly with local authorities, the voluntary sector, and private entities to build stronger support networks. By pooling resources and knowledge, we ensure marginalized voices are heard and comprehensive support structures are put in place.

  • ✦ Collaborative Initiatives
  • ✦ Resource Sharing Networks

Future Rehabilitation Programme

Beloved Berachah is currently developing a comprehensive, long-term rehabilitation initiative. This programme will offer structured, intensive support to help women fundamentally rebuild their lives, rediscover their identities, and achieve lasting transformation.

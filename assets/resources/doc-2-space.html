Preparing Your Space

When supporting vulnerable women, the environment speaks volumes before a single word is said. Use this checklist to ensure your space is physically and emotionally safe.

Physical Environment

  • Lighting & Layout: Are spaces well-lit? Are chairs arranged so no one feels trapped or blocked from an exit?
  • Quiet Spaces: Is there a designated quiet area for someone who may become overwhelmed or triggered?
  • Signage: Are exits and bathrooms clearly marked? Predictability greatly reduces anxiety.

Emotional Environment

  • Welcoming Language: Do greeters and volunteers use warm, non-judgmental language?
  • Clear Expectations: Are the schedules or agendas for meetings/workshops clear so attendees know exactly what to expect?
  • Visible Resources: Are national helpline numbers and safeguarding policies visibly displayed?
